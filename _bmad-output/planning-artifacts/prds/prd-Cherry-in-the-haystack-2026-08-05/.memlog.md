- (decision) Q1 VISION ANSWERED: visitors get curated information to rapidly EXPLORE and COMPARE the tools actually in use in a given category. Core value is NOT a tool list - it is reducing the cost of discovery, comparison and initial vetting by putting what you need in one place. Per-tool fields: name+icon, capability summary, category/purpose, links (official site / GitHub / docs / product page), public interest metrics such as GitHub stars, license type AND commercial-usability, recency of update, verification status and cautions. Five questions a visitor must be able to answer fast: what is widely used in this category, what problem does each solve, is it still maintained, can I use it in a personal or company project, are there license/security/privacy issues needing further checking
- (decision) Q2 VERIFIED DEFINED: 'verified' explicitly does NOT warrant safety or quality. It means that AS OF COLLECTION TIME the tool's existence, accessibility, maintenance state, usage evidence and absence of serious risk were checked. This framing is deliberate and limits liability
- (decision) Q2 HARD-FAIL (any one removes 'verified'): official site/repo/docs unreachable; project or service discontinued or deleted; the tool's existence or official source cannot be confirmed; clear evidence of spam, impersonation or malware distribution; confirmed unresolved serious vulnerability or breach; opaque personal-data collection with NO deletion/refusal/opt-out mechanism; clear evidence of license violation, copyright infringement or unauthorized redistribution
- (decision) Q2 DOWNGRADE (flag, do not remove): long dormant with unclear maintenance; too little usage evidence (users, stars, downloads); missing or unusable docs; early-stage or experimental; recent security issue with unconfirmed resolution; repeated outages or unstable operation; unclear privacy policy or data-retention policy. Status vocabulary suggested by user: needs-review / caution / possibly-inactive
- (decision) Q2 AGE RULE: an old tool is NOT excluded merely for being old. Judge on recency of update, current usability, user activity and official support, taken together
- (decision) Q3 ANSWERED: BOTH perspectives, and they must be SEPARATE FIELDS. catalog_use = may WE collect/store/process/display this metadata, name, icon, description, links and public metrics (checks: icon/logo usage rights, quoting or rewriting official copy, GitHub and site public-metadata collection, license/ToS data-reuse terms, crawling restrictions, attribution or permission requirements). end_user_use = may a VISITOR use the tool for company work, paid products, client projects or revenue-generating work (checks: OSS commercial-use permission, free vs paid tier commercial terms, distribution/modification/resale/hosting/SaaS limits, user-count or revenue or company-size thresholds, ownership and commercial terms of generated OUTPUT, whether an extra contract or enterprise license is needed). - (decision) PRIVACY (tension 3 resolved): opacity is DOWNGRADED from hard-fail to warning, because automated adjudication is costly and error-prone. HARD FAIL only on reliably evidenced: malicious or deceptive data collection; explicit refusal of deletion requests with no legal/contractual basis; disclosing sensitive data to third parties without consent; a serious unresolved privacy breach; official sanction or credible evidence of repeated policy violation in practice; services designed for malware, credential theft or exfiltration. WARN/REVIEW: no findable privacy policy; unclear retention; unclear training-data use; opt-out or deletion path hard to confirm; undocumented enterprise security options; stale or self-contradictory policy docs. Governing principle: UNCLEAR IS A WARNING, CONFIRMED SERIOUS HARM IS A FAIL.
