The through-line

The discovery net worked. Every quality mechanism the project designed lives in stages that never ran.

The repository split into two halves. The discovery half — harvesting entities and cases — is complete, tested, and pushed: 1,161 entities and 231 cases on disk. The vetting half — validate, select, build the deck — exists only as specs, three of them uncommitted on one machine, and none of it has ever executed.

Every problem the deep-dives surface traces back to that one fact. Low-tier sources, the misleading "verified" label, the empty gate, the missing star counts — each is a place where a designed safeguard was written but never switched on. The fix across the board is not more harvesting; it is turning on the vetting that already exists, after pointing it at better inputs.
